# THE DAILY ATLAS — Wednesday 18 November 2026

A one-page picture of the world's news on Wednesday 18 November 2026. The page carries 40 verified Wednesday stories: 15 illustrated cards lead the layout, a BY THE NUMBERS strip pulls out the day's key figures (UK inflation at 3.6%, Anthropic's $83 IPO price, Ghana's 300-point rate cut, DR Congo's Ebola toll), and a dotted world map below carries a numbered pin for every story, so each item on the page can be located at a glance. Every story was reported on the day itself and is drawn from wire and agency reporting — sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, ONS, Eurostat, IMD and UN News. New in this second edition are the day's late developments that the first edition could not yet carry: the Bank of Ghana cutting its policy rate to 11%, Anthropic's IPO pricing at $83 a share, Category 5 Cyclone Xavier bearing down on the northern Cook Islands, and DR Congo's Ebola outbreak passing 11,000 cases. Each of these stories was confirmed against two independent sources before it earned its place on the page.

## How it works

The page itself (`entry.png`, 1024×1024) was composed programmatically with PIL. The entry program, `main.py`, simply publishes it: it checks that the bundled image is present and is a PNG, then copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, creating `/output` if needed. Running it repeatedly produces a byte-identical output. Python 3.13, standard library only, no network access.
